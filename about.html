Why Europe Made Easy?

Simple, convenient and hassle-free travel through Europe for Jewish and kosher travelers.

Made for Jewish and kosher travelers

Europe Made Easy was created to make traveling through Europe simple, convenient, and hassle-free for Jewish and kosher travelers.

Whether you're passing through Europe on a stopover, visiting for a day or two, or enjoying a longer vacation, we help you discover the major attractions and iconic sights of Europe's most famous cities without having to worry about the arrangements.

Our experiences cover the major highlights of each city, but we can also tailor the trip to individual preferences, interests, and special requests.

Our goal is simple: You make the memories. We take care of the details.

We take care of everything, including:

  • Private transportation and airport transfers
  • Professional and Jewish tour guides
  • Kosher meals and restaurant arrangements
  • Hotels and accommodation
  • Museum and attraction tickets
  • Sightseeing tours and itineraries
  • Boat trips, cruises, and special activities
  • All other travel planning and logistics
